## What is not open source

- **The photographs.** Every photograph the site shows (the images themselves are served from Google Drive and are not stored here), and everything in this repository made from them, such as the depth maps in `depth/`.
- **The writing.** Field notes, species notes, outing summaries, captions and newsletter dispatches, including `data/field-notes.json`, `data/outing-notes.json` and `data/dispatches.json`.
- **The photo index.** `data/gallery.json`: the archive's records (identifications, dates, ratings, colour palettes and the small blurred previews of each photograph) and the link-preview pages built from it in `p/`, `s/`, `o/` and `r/`.
